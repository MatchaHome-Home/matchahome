How Matcha Home works

We’re a platform that makes it easy for families and independent Pros to connect. You hire the Pro directly. We provide the tools.

1. Find the right Pro
Browse or request help for childcare, house resets, meal prep, laundry, or errands.
2. Book flexible hours
Usually in 2-hour blocks. Pros set their own rates and availability.
3. Secure payment
Pay through the platform. We take a small percentage; the rest goes directly to the Pro.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Matcha Home work?
We connect families with independent Pros. You hire the Pro directly. We provide matching tools, messaging, and secure payment processing.
Are Pros employees of Matcha Home?
No. Pros are independent contractors. Families contract directly with them. Matcha Home is the platform that facilitates the connection.
What does it cost?
Pros set their own rates. Matcha Home takes a percentage of each completed booking. Exact pricing details will be shared with waitlist members.
How is this different from a babysitter?
You get flexible support for the whole home (not just the kids) and can build Your Circle of trusted Pros you rebook easily.
