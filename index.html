Who Matcha Home is for

Who want flexible 2-hour blocks of light childcare + household support without managing multiple people or long-term contracts.
Not the right fit

Independent Pros
Helpers who want to set their own rates, choose their hours, and build lasting relationships with a small circle of families.

How it works

We’re the platform that makes connecting simple. Families hire Pros directly. We provide the tools for matching, messaging, and secure payments.

1. Find the right Pro
Browse or request help for childcare, house resets, meal prep, laundry, or errands.
2. Book flexible hours
Usually in 2-hour blocks. Pros set their own rates and availability.
3. Pay securely
Payment goes through the platform. We take a small percentage; the rest goes directly to the Pro.
Coming soon for founding members: Live availability calendar, ratings & reviews, and one-tap rebooking from Your Circle.

How payments work

Simple and transparent. Matcha Home is the platform — not the employer.

Pros set their rates
You see the full price before you book. Example: a Pro sets $32/hour.
Small platform fee
We take a percentage of each completed booking to cover tools and secure payments. The exact percentage will be shared with waitlist members.
Pro gets paid directly
The remainder goes straight to the independent Pro after the booking is completed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Matcha Home work?
We connect families with independent Pros. You hire the Pro directly. We provide matching tools, messaging, and secure payment processing.
Are Pros employees of Matcha Home?
No. Pros are independent contractors. Families contract directly with them. Matcha Home is the platform that facilitates the connection only.
What does it cost?
Pros set their own rates. Matcha Home takes a percentage of each completed booking. Exact details will be shared with waitlist members.
How is this different from a babysitter?
You get flexible support for the whole home (not just the kids) and can build Your Circle of trusted Pros you rebook easily.
