FAQ

Are Pros employees of Matcha Home?
No. Pros are independent contractors. Families contract directly with them.
How do payments work?
Pros set their rates. Matcha Home takes a percentage of each completed booking. Exact details will be shared with waitlist members.
How is this different from a regular babysitter?
You get flexible support for the whole home and can build a small circle of Pros you rebook easily, with verification and reliability signals.
