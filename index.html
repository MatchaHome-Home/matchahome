Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?
You connect with independent Pros through the platform. Most families build a small circle of trusted Pros they rebook. Matcha Home provides the tools for matching, messaging, and secure payments. You contract directly with the Pro.
What does it cost?
Pricing details are coming soon — join the waitlist for early access. The platform takes a percentage of completed transactions to cover matching tools and payment processing.
How is this different from a babysitter?
You get flexible support for the whole home (not just the kids) and can build Your Circle of trusted Pros. Pros set their own rates and availability. Matcha Home is the platform that helps you connect — not the employer.
