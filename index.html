FAQ

How does the free trial work?

Early adopters get 60 days of Plus free. After the trial ends you can continue or drop to Lite with no charge.

How do payments work?

You and the Pro agree on the rate. You pay the Pro directly. Matcha Home charges a small service fee on completed bookings.

Is this for full-time help?

No. Matcha Home is designed for flexible, part-time support — not full-time nannying or full-time household staff.
