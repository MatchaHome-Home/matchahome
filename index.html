FAQ

How does Matcha Home work?
We connect families with independent Pros. You hire the Pro directly. We provide the tools.
Are Pros employees?
No. Pros are independent contractors. Families contract directly with them.
How do payments work?
You pay the Pro directly. Matcha Home charges a small service fee on completed bookings.
What is Companion Care?
It’s non-medical support for seniors so family caregivers can take a break. A Pro can offer company, conversation, and light help while you’re out. Pros do not provide medical care, medication management, or hands-on personal care.
Can I book just once?
Yes. Book for a single evening, a few hours, or ongoing support.
