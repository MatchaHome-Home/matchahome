Frequently asked questions

Who is the employer?

Neither. Matcha Home is only a marketplace. Independent Pros are not employees of Matcha Home or of the clients. Clients hire and pay Pros directly.

How do payments work?

You and the Pro agree on the rate. You pay the Pro directly. Matcha Home charges a small service fee on completed bookings.

What’s the difference between ID Verified and LiveScan Verified?

ID Verified is basic identity confirmation. LiveScan Verified is an optional higher tier that includes additional background screening for Pros who want it.

Do you provide insurance?

No. Matcha Home does not provide insurance or employment benefits. Clients and Pros are responsible for their own arrangements.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Yes. Because you book directly with the Pro, you arrange any changes or cancellations with them according to what you both agree on.
