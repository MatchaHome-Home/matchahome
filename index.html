Frequently Asked Questions

How does a membership work?
You get hours to use whenever you want. Most families set a weekly schedule with a consistent Pro, and ad-hoc support is also available. We handle all the scheduling and payments.
What does it cost?
Memberships are monthly with no long-term contract. You can pause or cancel as your needs change. Pricing details coming soon — join the waitlist for early access.
How is this different from a babysitter?
We take on the work of finding, vetting, and coordinating. You get flexible care for the whole home, not just the kids, and you can build a bench of favorites.
