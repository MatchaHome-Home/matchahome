Frequently Asked Questions

How does a membership work?
You receive hours to use whenever you need them. Most families develop a regular rhythm with one or two Pros, and can also request additional support as needed. We handle scheduling and payments.
What does it cost?
Memberships are monthly with no long-term contract. You can pause or cancel as your needs change. Pricing details are coming soon — join the waitlist for early access.
How is this different from a babysitter?
We take on the work of finding, vetting, and coordinating. You get flexible support for the whole home, not just the kids, and the chance to build a small bench of trusted Pros.
