Questions people ask

How does a membership actually work?
You get hours you can use whenever you need them. Most families end up with a loose weekly rhythm with one or two Pros they like, and can also request last-minute help. We handle the scheduling and payments.
What does it cost?
It’s a monthly membership with no long-term contract. You can pause or cancel if life changes. We’re still figuring out the exact pricing — join the waitlist and I’ll share the details first.
How is this different from just finding a babysitter?
We do the work of finding, vetting, and coordinating. You get help for the whole house, not only the kids, and you can build a small group of people you actually know.
