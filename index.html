Frequently asked questions

Who is the employer?

Neither. Matcha Home is only a marketplace. Independent Pros are not employees of Matcha Home or of the clients. Clients hire and pay Pros directly.

What is the difference between Lite and Plus?

Lite is free and lets you browse and send limited messages. Plus unlocks unlimited messaging, Your Circle, real-time availability, and priority booking. Early adopters receive 60 days of Plus free.

What happens after the 60-day trial?

Plus continues only if you choose to stay. You can opt out anytime before the trial ends and drop to Lite with no charge.

How do payments work?

You and the Pro agree on the rate. You pay the Pro directly. Matcha Home charges a small service fee on completed bookings.

Is this for full-time help?

No. Matcha Home is designed for flexible, part-time support — not full-time nannying or full-time household staff.
