How Matcha Home works

A simple marketplace that connects you with independent contractors. No employment relationship is created. Pros set their own rates. You pay them directly. We take a small percentage of the transaction.

1. Create an account

Tell us what kind of help you need and your preferred schedule.

2. Browse available Pros

See who is available, their experience, and verification level.

3. Connect and book directly

Message the Pro, agree on details, and pay them directly. Matcha Home is not the employer or the service provider.
