Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you join the waitlist or apply to be a Pro, we collect the information you provide (such as name, email, phone number, and any details you choose to share about your needs or experience). Once the platform launches, we will also collect information related to accounts, bookings, and payments.

How we use information

We use the information to contact you about Matcha Home, operate the platform, process payments (once available), improve the service, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the platform (for example, payment processors) or as required by law. We will not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us through the waitlist form. Once accounts are live, you will have additional controls inside the platform.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent through the waitlist form on the homepage.
