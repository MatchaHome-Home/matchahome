Independent Pro Agreement

Last updated: November 2026 (draft — for review only)

This agreement confirms that you are an independent contractor, not an employee of Matcha Home.

1. Independent contractor status

You are an independent contractor. You are not an employee, partner, or agent of Matcha Home. You are free to accept or decline bookings, set your own rates and availability, and work with other platforms or clients.

2. Services

You decide which services you offer and the scope of work you are comfortable performing. Families hire you directly. Matcha Home only facilitates the connection, messaging, and payment.

3. Payments

You set your own rates. Matcha Home may deduct a platform fee from completed bookings. The remaining amount is paid to you. Exact fee details will be disclosed before you receive bookings.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance (if desired), and compliance with applicable laws. You agree to provide services safely and professionally and to communicate clearly with families about scope and expectations.

5. Verification

You may choose to complete optional ID and background check verification. Completion of verification may be displayed on your profile.

6. Termination

Either you or Matcha Home may end your participation on the platform at any time. Outstanding payments for completed bookings will still be processed according to the payment terms.

Contact

Questions about this agreement can be sent through the Pro application form.
